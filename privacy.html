TotalMotion — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

TotalMotion (“the app”) is designed to be private by default.

Health data

With your permission, the app reads workout and health data from Apple Health (for example workouts, distance, heart rate, steps, flights climbed, and sleep) to display summaries and insights. The app is read-only — it never writes to or modifies your Apple Health data.

Where your data lives

All data processed by the app stays on your device. Your notes, tags, and gear are stored in your own private iCloud account using Apple’s iCloud/CloudKit. This information is accessible only to you through your Apple ID. The developer cannot access it.

No data collection

The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal or health data. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies in the app.

In-app purchase

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. The app does not receive your payment information.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
