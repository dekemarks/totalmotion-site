TotalMotion Support

TotalMotion aggregates and summarizes the workouts already in Apple Health — with goals, charts, insights, and more.

Get help

Questions, bugs, or feature requests? Email dekemarks@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you.

Common questions

Why don’t I see my activities? Make sure you allowed TotalMotion to read your health data (Settings › Privacy & Security › Health), and that your activities have synced to Apple Health.

Does TotalMotion change my Health data? No — it’s read-only and never writes to Apple Health.

How does the purchase work? The app is free for 7 days, then a one-time purchase unlocks it on all your devices. Use “Restore Purchase” if you’ve bought it before.
