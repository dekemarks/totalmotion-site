Frequently asked questions

Why don't I see my activities?

Make sure you allowed TotalMotion to read your health data: iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › TotalMotion, and turn on the categories. Also confirm your workouts have synced into Apple Health. Pull down on the Activities list to refresh.

Why do my iPhone and iPad show different activities?

Each device reads from its own copy of Apple Health, and Apple syncs Health data between devices over iCloud — which can lag from minutes to hours. If a workout is on your iPhone but not yet your iPad, the iPad is simply waiting on Apple's Health sync (make sure Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Health is on for both). Once it arrives, reopen TotalMotion to see it.

The same workout was recorded by my Apple Watch and my Oura ring — will it show twice?

No. When two sources record the same session, TotalMotion merges them into one (preferring the richest/primary source) so your totals aren't doubled. You can turn this off with Settings › Merge Duplicates, and you can manually hide or un-hide any activity.

Why don't the numbers exactly match Apple Health or Oura?

A few honest reasons: TotalMotion's "Today" distance, steps, calories, and flights are all-day totals (everyday movement, what Apple Fitness shows), while an individual activity shows just that workout. Different devices also measure slightly differently. For sleep, when more than one device logs the same night, TotalMotion uses the single source that recorded the most sleep and counts overlapping time once — so it should closely match the Health app's merged number.

How does the sleep breakdown work?

Open Insights › Sleep and tap in for the detail: a per-night hypnogram (Awake / REM / Core / Deep) you can swipe between nights, and a weekly time-of-day view. Stages come straight from Apple Health; a source that only reports generic "asleep" time is shown as Core.

What counts as a "flight" of stairs?

Your iPhone/Apple Watch barometer counts roughly every 10 feet (~3 m) of elevation gained on foot as one flight — the same metric Apple Health uses. Walking uphill can add flights too.

In the Custom Chart, why can the second (overlay) series only be a line or area, not bars?

The primary series is usually bars; two bar series on the same plot sit in the same spots and hide each other. So the overlay is a line or area (the classic "bars + line" combo). The second series is drawn on a right-hand axis scaled to its own range.

How do goals and streaks work?

Set recurring goals in Settings › Goals. They're grouped into General Activity goals — distance, steps, calories, and flights, which use your all-day movement and count even on days without a logged workout — and Logged Activity goals — activities, duration, and elevation, which come from workouts you've recorded. The Today rings show your progress.

Goals are measured as a daily pace: if you pick a weekly, monthly, or yearly period, the target is divided by 7, 30, or 365 to get a daily amount. Your Goal Streak (in Insights) counts each consecutive day you met all enabled goals at that daily pace — and past days lock in, so changing a goal later never rewrites history.

What's the difference between "All Stats" and "All Activities" in Summary?

All Stats (the default) shows your everyday movement and goals — including days where you didn't record a workout — so steps, distance, calories, and flights come from all-day totals. All Activities shows only your logged workouts across every type, and a specific type (e.g. Running) narrows it further. The Today tab's "This Week" comparison works the same way, split into General Activity (all-day) and Logged Activity (workouts), with a toggle to compare against the same point last week or last week's full total.

Why isn't my widget updating in real time?

iOS — not the app — controls how often widgets refresh, and widgets can't read Apple Health on their own. TotalMotion updates the widget whenever you open the app, and in the background roughly once an hour as you move. So the numbers are current but not second-by-second; open the app for an instant refresh. At midnight the rings reset for the new day.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export a single activity as GPX (with route + heart rate) from its detail screen, or export your whole activity list as CSV from the Activities tab. You can also share an activity as an image card.

How do the widgets work?

Add the Home Screen or Lock Screen widget to see your daily goal rings at a glance. Choose which goals appear on the Lock Screen in Settings › Goals.

How does pricing work? What about my other devices?

TotalMotion is free for a 7-day trial, then a single one-time purchase unlocks it forever — no subscription. It unlocks on all your devices signed in to the same Apple Account; tap Restore Purchase on another device if needed.

Is my data private?

Yes. TotalMotion is read-only and never writes to Apple Health. Your data stays on your device and your own private iCloud — it's never sent to the developer. See the Privacy Policy.
